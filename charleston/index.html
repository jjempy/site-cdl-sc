Charleston Tri-County CDL: a port-freight market with three verified providers, not a list copied from an aggregator

North Charleston is a port and distribution market, and the provider pages here draw students from Goose Creek, Ladson, Hanahan, Summerville, and Moncks Corner. This page covers Charleston, Berkeley, and Dorchester counties. No standalone Dorchester County CDL provider was verified in this build — that gap is stated here, not filled with an aggregator name.

Local evidence that makes this page different from a city swap

  • Trident's CDL program page says the program is based at its Berkeley Campus, while a separate Trident Berkeley page says the program will temporarily move to Thornley Campus, North Charleston, during renovation. Do not enroll on the basis of a campus address from this page alone — confirm the current training site with Trident first.
  • TransTech's Charleston campus is at Meeting Street Road, North Charleston, and its page lists Goose Creek, Ladson, Hanahan, Summerville, and Moncks Corner as its draw area, with SC Works approval and WIOA office cooperation.
  • Miller-Motte's Summerville MMDI site is a separate site from the Miller-Motte Charleston campus, which multiple sources report as permanently closed in December 2024 and which does not appear on Miller-Motte's current campus list. This page does not list the closed Charleston campus as current.
  • Providers found only on aggregators in this corridor — Lowcountry Truck Driving School and Charleston Truck Driving Academy, with aggregator prices — are not listed here, because no provider-owned page was found for them in this build. Palmetto Training is also not listed as verified, because its own site returned no extractable content and its aggregator addresses conflict.
