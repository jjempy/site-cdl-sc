Local evidence that makes this page different from a city swap

  • Midlands Tech delivers its CDL program with Ancora Education, and its SC Path listing reads WIOA "No" for this program — a funding fact that must be verified with the school and local board before any WIOA claim is made about Midlands.
  • Roadmaster and TransTech Columbia both have SC Path figures ($7,695 and $4,154, both WIOA Eligible: Yes on SC Path) that are not published on their own Columbia pages. This page labels those as workforce listings, not provider prices, and does not present them as quotes.
  • 160 Driving Academy of Columbia has conflicting addresses in listings (Two Notch Road vs. Millwood Avenue). No street address is pinned here until the current address is confirmed. Its 160-hour figure comes from the national program description, not a Columbia-specific page, and is labeled as such in the data sheet.
  • Out-of-county schools that appear in aggregator results for Columbia — including Lexington County schools in West Columbia and Lexington, and Sumter-area schools — are excluded from this corridor, not counted.
