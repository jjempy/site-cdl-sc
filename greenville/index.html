Local evidence that makes this page different from a city swap

  • Greenville Tech's program is delivered with Ancora Education as training partner, the same partner model as Midlands Tech in Columbia. Registration routes through Ancora and a course-schedule system, which is why a gross price and dated next start are not published on the program page in this build.
  • Greenville Tech's Truck Driver Training (CDL) is listed on the college's 2026–2027 Career Pathways Scholarship eligible-programs list, with no CDL-specific amount published on that list.
  • Greer schools are not filed here as Greenville County listings, because Greer straddles Greenville and Spartanburg counties and the published addresses do not confirm the county side. ACE Driving Academy in Greer and SBL Truck Driving Academy in Greer are held in the data sheet pending county confirmation, not counted as Greenville rows.
  • Out-of-county schools that appear in aggregator results for Greenville — including Duncan in Spartanburg County and Tri-County Technical College in the Anderson area — are excluded from this corridor, not counted.
